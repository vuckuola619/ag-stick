## 🕵️ 1. Brand Concept: "Stickman Cinematic Realism"

The channel merges two powerful psychological elements:
1. **The Iconic Stickman**: Minimalist solid white circular head (`#FFFFFF`), pure geometric simplicity, expressive cartoon eyes, clean silhouette recognition.
2. **Hollywood Cinematic Realism**: Physical textures (wool trench coat, brass optical glass, aged parchment, tungsten desk lamps), 35mm anamorphic depth of field, chiaroscuro shadows, volumetric haze, and **zero AI slop** (no mangled hands, no distorted gibberish text, no cluttered plastic surfaces).
