## 🕵️ 3. Mascot & Character Anatomy: "The Investigator"

The channel's recurring host and visual anchor across thumbnails, avatars, and scenes:
* **Head**: Solid blank white circle (`#FFFFFF`), pure circular geometry, no nose, no mouth unless expressing extreme shock.
* **Eyes**: Simple expressive wide cartoon oval eyes with thick black ink pupils. Capable of intense focus, squinting suspicion, or wide shock.
* **Attire**: 1940s vintage detective trench coat (dark charcoal/tan) with popped collar, dark tie, and tilted black fedora hat with a yellow/cyan ribbon band.
* **Limbs**: Bold, clean black stick limbs (`8–10px stroke`) wearing black leather gloves.
* **Signature Props**: Brass magnifying glass, glowing lantern, top-secret red folder stamped `CLASSIFIED`, antique nautical compass.
* **Style Constraints**: 
  - 100% flat cel-shading and hand-drawn comic ink outlines.
  - **Strictly NO 3D CGI**, no photorealism, no realistic human faces, no chrome ray-tracing.

## 💡 5. Launch Slate: Top 10 High-CTR Episode Concepts

1. **The Lead Masks Case (1966)** — Two electronics technicians found dead on a Rio de Janeiro hilltop wearing formal suits and handmade lead eye masks, with notes instructing them to "wait for the signal."
2. **The Flannan Isles Lighthouse Vanishing (1900)** — Three veteran keepers vanished from an isolated Scottish rock with uneaten meals, an overturned chair, and a final log entry about an unnatural storm that never occurred.
3. **The Tunguska Event (1908)** — A shockwave 1,000 times more powerful than Hiroshima flattened 80 million trees in Siberian wilderness, leaving zero impact craters.
4. **The Voynich Manuscript** — A 600-year-old cipher book written in an unreadable language with botanical drawings of plants that do not exist on Earth.
5. **The Lost Airship of the Arctic: Italia (1928)** — The tragic crash of Umberto Nobile's red tent expedition onto drifting pack ice and the desperate radio distress signals from the edge of the world.
6. **The Ourang Medan Ghost Ship (1947)** — A cargo ship found adrift in the Strait of Malacca where every crew member and dog died with frozen wide eyes staring into the empty sky.
7. **The Mary Celeste Mystery (1872)** — An undamaged merchant vessel discovered sailing with full sails, six months of rations, and personal belongings completely undisturbed—but not a single soul on board.
8. **The Antikythera Mechanism** — A 2,000-year-old bronze computer pulled from a Greek shipwreck that calculated planetary orbits with precision centuries ahead of its time.
9. **The Devil's Sea (Dragon's Triangle)** — Japan's mysterious Pacific Bermuda Triangle where research vessels and bombers vanished into sudden electronic fog.
10. **The Philadelphia Experiment Legend** — Declassified WWII naval degaussing trials that sparked rumors of a destroyer escort teleporting into hyperspace.
