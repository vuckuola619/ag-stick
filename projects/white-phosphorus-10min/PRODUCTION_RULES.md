# Neon Atom Production Rules — Visual & Motion Graphics Standards

## 🚫 Critical Directive: ZERO AI Typography on Images (Strict Anti-Slop)

Starting immediately and strictly enforced for all upcoming episodes:

1. **NO Text in AI Prompts**:
   - **NEVER** instruct the image generator to render text, comic callouts, banners, titles, or words inside the artwork (e.g., NO *"PEE TO FERTILIZER!"*, NO *"1,500 GALLONS?!"*, NO *"STINK INTENSE!"*).
   - AI typography looks generic ("AI slop"), produces garbled glyphs, and collides with Remotion HUD overlays, badges, and timecodes.

2. **100% Programmatic Typography in Remotion**:
   - All headlines, kinetic hooks, chapter titles, statistics, badges, and subtitles must be rendered dynamically in **Remotion React components**.
   - Use clean modern typography (`Inter`, `Montserrat`, `JetBrains Mono`), crisp CSS shadows, glassmorphism containers, and programmatic entry/exit spring animations.

3. **Pure Visual Scene Composition (16:9 Widescreen Only)**:
   - Aspect ratio must always be **16:9 widescreen (1672×941 or 1920×1080)**.
   - Framing must be **center-staged** or balanced across cinematic rule-of-thirds.
   - Character consistency: Minimalist stick figure with a solid white circular head (`#ffffff`), simple expressive round cartoon eyes, thin black stick limbs, era-appropriate attire, bold clean outlines, and rich cel-shading.
